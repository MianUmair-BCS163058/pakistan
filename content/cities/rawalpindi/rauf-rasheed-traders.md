Introduction: ‎Rauf Rasheed Traders is a key player in Pakistan's agricultural trade, specializing in the bulk trading of rice and pulses within the Punjab, Kashmir and KPK region. We source high-quality grains and pulses to cater the needs of our Commercial and Home Clients. At Rauf Rasheed Traders we have our very own processing plants to ensure quality standards.
